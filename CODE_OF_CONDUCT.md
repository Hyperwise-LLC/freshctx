# Code of Conduct

FreshCtx contributors must communicate respectfully, evaluate ideas on technical merit, and maintain an environment free from harassment or discrimination.

Project maintainers may edit or remove abusive, threatening, discriminatory, intentionally disruptive, or privacy-violating content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner. This concise policy should be replaced with a named community standard and dedicated reporting contact before broad community launch.
